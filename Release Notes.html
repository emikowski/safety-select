// Release Notes & Platform Overview

Release Notes

A complete overview of the GVS Safety Intelligence platform — from access and language support to the technical and commercial modules. Each block groups the capabilities of one area of the product.

1
// Access & Security

Authentication & Privacy

A local-first access layer that gates the platform and handles user data under LGPD.

1.1Account creation & sign-inEmail/password sign-up and login, plus Google sign-in. The first registered account becomes the administrator.
1.2LGPD consentExplicit consent to the Privacy Policy is required at sign-up, with the full policy available on screen.
1.3Local-first storageUser data and query history are stored locally in the device browser; in this prototype no data is sent to external servers.
1.4Session persistenceThe platform remembers the signed-in user and the last screen between visits.
2
// Internationalization

Multilingual Platform (PT · EN · ES)

The entire platform operates in Portuguese, English and Spanish.

2.1Language selectionSwitch language at any time from the selector available on the start screen — PT, EN or ES — and the whole interface updates instantly.
2.2Full content translationInterface labels, product descriptions and certifications are localized, including the catalog item descriptions delivered per language.
2.3Locale-aware formattingDates, reference codes and on-screen messaging follow the conventions of each selected locale.
3
// Guided Configuration · Technical

Guided Equipment Configurator

A step-by-step builder that leads the user from their need to a complete, compatible respirator configuration.

3.1Two entry pathsStart by activity — describe where you work and get recommended lines — or by product type when the line is already known.
3.2Activity-based recommendationIndustrial activities are mapped to the best-suited product lines, so the catalog narrows to what actually fits the application.
3.3Step-by-step flowA clear sequence — Line → Unit → Head-top → Sensor → Accessories — with progress indication at every step.
3.4Compatibility filteringOnly items compatible with the chosen unit and head-top are shown, preventing invalid combinations.
4
// Product Catalog · Technical

Lines, Head-tops & Accessories

The full GVS / RPB respiratory portfolio, structured for selection.

4.1Respiratory linesPowered air (PAPR · PX5), supplied-air and gas-detection systems organized into selectable product lines.
4.2Head-top familiesZ-Link, Z4, T-Link, T200, Nova 3 and Astro — each family with its own set of models (SKUs) and variants.
4.3Accessories, parts & consumablesHundreds of items classified by type, each linked to the head-tops and units it serves.
4.4Real catalog dataCodes, descriptions, certifications and product images sourced directly from the GVS / RPB catalog.
5
// Media & Documentation

Imagery, Video & Certifications

Every product is presented with the material a buyer needs to decide.

5.1Product imageryEach unit, head-top and accessory displays its real product photo.
5.2Demo videosA watch-video modal is available on products that have demonstration footage.
5.3CertificationsCompliance and certification information is shown per item where applicable.
6
// Commercial Module

Quote Builder

The configuration is assembled into a clear proposal ready to send to GVS.

6.1Configuration cartAdd or remove items and adjust quantities, with a live item count throughout the flow.
6.2Live summaryA full review of the selected items — main product, sensors, accessories and consumables — before sending.
6.3Reference codeEach submission generates a unique reference code for tracking and follow-up.
7
// Lead Capture & Sync · Commercial

Submission & CRM Pipeline

A compliant lead-capture step records every configuration for the commercial team.

7.1Contact formCaptures name, company, role, phone, e-mail and free-text notes alongside the configuration.
7.2LGPD-compliant consentExplicit authorization to be contacted is required before a configuration can be submitted.
7.3Google Sheets integrationEach submission is written to a Google Sheet through a secure Apps Script Web App, with write-locking and automatic retries so no lead is lost.
7.4Specialist follow-upThe recorded lead reaches a GVS specialist, who follows up with the proposal.
